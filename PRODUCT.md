# DSH Medals

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Individual DSH users collecting personal achievements across locally readable sessions and models.

## Product Purpose
Turn sustained exploration and token usage into a progressively rewarding personal collection.

## Operating Context
Installed in the DSH web profile, including DS Bot and dsh-web-mobile. A native sidebar entry opens the collection in the main content panel. A fixed lower-left button returns to the exact conversation it was opened from.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Product terminology is 挑战, never 任务 in this plugin's UI.
- All users share fixed challenge criteria: daily medals at 5M/10M/20M tokens, with 5M qualifying a day for consistency. Lifetime usage uses absolute thresholds.
- Daily repeats accumulate counts; weekly/monthly records retain their individual dates.
- Consistency is the main path. Token volume and period growth are parallel progression paths.
- Existing earned medals remain permanently collectible. Token history remains eligible for backfill. Consistency tracking begins when challenges are enabled.
- All models/providers: uncached input + output + cache read/write; reasoning is not double-counted. Fixed initial timezone, Monday-start weeks, calendar months.
- No leaderboard, account-wide data assumption, or forced celebration during conversation.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the existing handcrafted high-detail medals. The gallery groups medals into series shelves (daily, weekly & monthly, qualified days, monthly usage, milestones & breakthroughs). Monthly usage tiers (200M/500M/1B/3B) are realistic celestial bodies — Moon, Earth, Sun, black hole — each carrying the previous tier as a companion, distinct from the abstract cosmic token milestones; new medals are announced by a gold prompt in the page header, and the featured stage slowly cycles through every earned medal, newest first, alongside the next goal. Add simpler, number-led collectible medals for easier challenges. Craft complexity progresses with challenge difficulty. Unlocked medals are real interactive 3D objects with metal and enamel. Locked medals show the outer case and recognizable internal motif lines, without full materials, reverse view or rotation.

Number-led medals serve daily and early consistency challenges. The 100-day edition is an evergreen tree with faceted jade leaves on a gold shield; its front and engraved reverse contain no numeral. The 365-day edition uses a compass. The 1B, 5B and 10B token milestones form a cosmic progression: Starfire (a beaded roundel of rays around an amber core), Star Chart (an eight-point frame around a graduated star chart) and River of Stars (a twelve-point double frame around a layered enamel galaxy); their tier appears only on the reverse. Each sculptural challenge has a distinct named motif, earnable through usage, growth and lifetime challenges. Challenges and earned records share one collection. A hidden, clearly labeled, read-only Debug preview (five taps on the gallery title) switches all medal appearances between earned and unearned states without changing real records.

## Product Principles
- Numbers lead ordinary tiers; sculptural motifs express rarer consistency milestones.
- Repeated ordinary achievements build toward long-term rare collections.
- Earned medals can be shared as a 4:5 image (copy or download) showing the medal, its record and current lifetime usage.
- Earned collections reveal full materials and front/back exploration; Debug previews are clearly distinguished from earned records.
- Earned records persist across upgrades and source-log deletion.
